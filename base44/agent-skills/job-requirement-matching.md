# Skill: job_requirement_matching

Comparison against a **real** job. Without a real target job this skill does not apply: a professional field alone is not a target job, and no job-alignment recommendation may be produced from one.

## Procedure

1. **Establish the requirements from real data only.** Use the advert text or the requirements the candidate provides. Never invent, extrapolate or assume requirements, and never present typical expectations of an occupation as this employer's requirements.
2. **Classify what the job asks for:**
   - **Explicit requirements** — stated as required.
   - **Preferred qualifications** — stated as advantageous.
   - **Keywords** — terminology worth mirroring for automated screening and for the reader.
   - **Gaps** — requirements the CV does not currently evidence.
   - **Matching strengths** — what the CV already evidences well for this role.
3. **Compare against what the CV actually states**, item by item, using the CV index.
4. **Formulate recommendations from real overlap.** Improve how existing, genuine experience is presented for this role. Never recommend adding a competence the candidate does not have, and never recommend claiming a requirement is met when it is not.
5. **Assess fit of a varied background against this role** by delegating to `positioning_and_career_narrative` — a different professional background may be an asset for the role, and that must be examined before it is treated as a gap.
6. **Report honest verdicts.** If the CV is genuinely strong for this job, say so rather than manufacturing cosmetic recommendations.

## Boundaries

- When and how to call a tool to retrieve an advert is a tool-level rule, not part of this skill.
- Job data informs the *problem* and the *terminology*. It never becomes a fact about the candidate.
- Recommendation types, severities and the output block are defined by the agent's contract, not here.
- Analysis only: no edits, no saving, no execution.
