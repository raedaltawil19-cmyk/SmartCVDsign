# Skill: evidence_pack_construction

Evidence is prepared **during** the review, not when the user clicks a recommendation. Evidence is diagnosis: it explains and supports a recommendation. It is never an execution step and never a CV write.

## Ordered procedure

1. **Inspect the complete relevant context.** Read the full CV data and CV index. Read every field of the target item and the items directly connected to it.
2. **Identify the actual target** — section, item and, where determinable, the specific field that must change. Use only identifiers that appear literally in the CV index. Never write the current value yourself; the app reads it from the CV.
3. **Derive before asking.** Determine what can reasonably be formulated from what already exists. If the existing material is sufficient for a useful improvement, prepare that improvement. Only what cannot be derived may be requested from the candidate — and the justification must state why derivation was not possible.
4. **Separate existing from missing.** What the target item already contains is *existing* material and must not reappear as missing or as something to confirm. What is missing must be named precisely — the specific fact that is absent, never a vague request such as "explain more".
5. **Keep related material genuinely related.** Only information with a direct, demonstrable connection to *this* problem and *this* item may be included as related material. Items from other sections, and unrelated skills, courses or languages, must never be used as evidence merely because they exist in the CV.
6. **Separate candidate facts from proposals and assumptions.** Facts stated in the CV are the candidate's. Anything you formulated is a proposal. Anything uncertain is an assumption. A link you suspect but cannot see stated — for instance that a particular training underlies a particular task — is an assumption and must be presented for the candidate's confirmation, never asserted.
7. **Draft only from confirmed material.** A proposed wording may be built solely from what the CV states plus what the candidate has confirmed. Never invent figures, results, responsibilities, certificates, courses, skills, proficiency levels, academic equivalences or experience. Never turn a recognition or validation claim into a domestic qualification.
8. **Ask only for what must be confirmed.** Confirmation is for *relationships and new claims* — a connection not stated in the CV, or material that would newly enter it. Never ask the candidate to confirm the existence of something they already listed.

## Sufficiency check before finishing

- Is the problem justified by something concretely present in the target item?
- Is every piece of existing material actually from that item?
- Is every piece of related material directly connected to this problem?
- Is each missing item a specific fact rather than a general question?
- Is the draft free of anything neither stated nor confirmed?
- Is nothing being requested that the CV already contains?

## Boundaries

- The evidence structure's field names, statuses and mandatory scope are defined by the agent's output contract. This skill fills that structure; it does not redefine it.
- No external research at this stage. General background knowledge is never evidence about the candidate.
- No writes, no execution, no claim that anything was applied.
