# Skill: positioning_and_career_narrative

A shared positioning skill for both general CV review and job-specific tailoring. Several professional backgrounds are **not** automatically a weakness, a lack of focus, or a reason to remove experience. Determine what the combination actually does for the candidate before recommending anything about it.

## Assessment

Assess each of the following, based on what the CV actually states:

1. **Coherence** — is there a thread that makes the path readable: a domain, a type of problem solved, a kind of responsibility, a progression?
2. **Transferable capabilities** — which capabilities carry across the different backgrounds and remain valuable in the current direction?
3. **Differentiation** — does the combination create something a single-track profile would not offer?
4. **Relevance to the target role** — only when a real target job is available, assess how the combination serves that role specifically using the job-tailoring workflow's established requirements and matching analysis. Without a real target job, do not invent role-specific relevance.
5. **Narrative strength** — is the story currently told well, or merely listed? Weak *presentation* of a strong combination is a presentation problem.

## Verdict and consequence

- **Diversity is valuable** (any of coherence, transferability, differentiation or role relevance holds) ⇒ recommend strengthening the positioning and the narrative: sharpen the professional identity, make the thread explicit, foreground the transferable capabilities and the differentiator. Do not recommend removing or hiding the experience.
- **Diversity genuinely harms clarity or fit** ⇒ the recommendation is about emphasis and presentation first: ordering, prominence, level of detail. Only recommend reducing or omitting an item where it demonstrably damages clarity or fit for the stated direction — and say plainly why.
- **Undetermined** ⇒ do not raise a positioning weakness at all.

The skill may support both agents, but it does not perform the job-ad parsing or requirement matching itself. In Job Tailor, it consumes established job relevance; in general CV Review, it focuses on coherence, transferability, differentiation and narrative strength.

Prefer strengthening a useful professional narrative over deleting useful experience. Never frame a career change, a varied path or a non-linear history as a defect in itself.

## Boundaries

- Assessment and recommendation wording only: no edits, no saving, no execution.
- Never invent a professional identity, level or ambition the CV does not support; a proposed narrative must be built from stated facts.
- Recommendation types and severities are defined by the agent's contract, not here.
- Length and form of any proposed text follow `content_shape_and_density`.
